01 / The Vision

Where structure
meets emotion.

A Pulse Between Us is a creative project envisioned by the minds of Third Year Live Visual Design and Production (LVD).

Inspired by the organic form of trees and the engineered geometry of truss, two branching structures reach towards one another, joining at their centre to create the unmistakable shape of a heart.

The installation explores the relationship between strength and vulnerability, individuality and unity — a visual reminder that connection can emerge from the structures we build together.
